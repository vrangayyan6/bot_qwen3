\- Education: Philosophy and Theology

\- Secular jobs: Actor, poet

\- Education: Theology

\- Secular jobs: None



\## Pope Paul VI (Giovanni Battista Montini)

\- Education: Theology, Canon Law

\- Secular jobs: Journalist (early career)
